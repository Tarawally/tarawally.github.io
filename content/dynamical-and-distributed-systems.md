AI has led to a major shift in the architecture and toolchain of distributed systems. Historically, the design of these systems centred on loosely coupled, scalable, and fault-tolerant microservices. Today, workloads are increasingly aligned with those seen in high-performance computing, especially for companies looking to leverage large datasets for analytical insights into customer behaviour, business trends, and automation.
